# Code of conduct

Participants should be respectful, specific and constructive. Welcome people with different backgrounds and experience. Critique code and behavior, not personal characteristics. Respect people's privacy and accessibility needs.

Harassment, discriminatory insults, threats, unwanted sexual attention, impersonation and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable. These expectations apply in issues, pull requests, discussions and other project-maintained spaces.

Maintainers may request edits, remove inappropriate content, limit participation or block accounts when necessary. Responses should be proportional, protect reporter privacy and give participants a chance to clarify mistakes where appropriate. Maintainers follow the same standards.

A private conduct-reporting contact has not yet been established. Ask the maintainer to arrange a private channel using a minimal contact request; do not post allegations or identifying details publicly. The release checklist requires establishing that contact before announcing the public community.
